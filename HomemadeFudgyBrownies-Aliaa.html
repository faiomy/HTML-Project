Homemade Fudgy Brownies

Rich, decadent, and incredibly fudgy chocolate brownies made simple for beginners.

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 10 mins
Servings 12 cookies
Calories 🔥 210 kcal

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda & pinch of salt
  • 3/4 cup chocolate chips

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Line: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cream butter and sugars: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until smooth.
  3. Add wet ingredients: Mix in the egg and vanilla extract until fully incorporated.
  4. Combine dry ingredients: Add flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir until a dough forms, then gently fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Bake & Cool: Scoop tablespoon-sized balls onto the sheet and bake for 10–12 minutes until edges are golden brown. Let cool before eating.
