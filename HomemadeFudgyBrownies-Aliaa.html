Homemade Fudgy Brownies

Rich, decadent, and incredibly fudgy chocolate brownies made simple for beginners.

Prep Time 15 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Servings 9 squares
Calories 🔥 280 kcal

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat & Prep: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Mix wet ingredients: In a medium bowl, mix the melted butter and sugar together until well combined. Add eggs and vanilla extract; beat until smooth.
  3. Add dry ingredients: Stir in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt just until combined. Do not overmix.
  4. Bake: Pour batter into the prepared pan and bake for 20 to 22 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with moist crumbs.
  5. Cool & Slice: Let them cool completely in the pan before cutting into squares and serving.
