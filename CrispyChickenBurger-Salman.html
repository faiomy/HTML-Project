Crispy Chicken Burger

Preparation: 15 minutes · Cooking: 15 minutes · Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breast pieces
  • 2 burger buns
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup flour
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • Lettuce and tomato
  • Cheese slices (optional)
  • Salt, pepper, and oil
  • Mayonnaise or burger sauce

How to Prepare

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Dip each piece in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
  3. Cook the chicken in a pan with oil until golden and fully cooked.
  4. Cut the burger buns in half and toast them lightly.
  5. Spread mayonnaise or burger sauce on the buns.
  6. Add the chicken, cheese, lettuce, and tomato.
  7. Close the burger and serve it hot.
